Services

Every wash, done properly.

From a quick exterior steam to a full inside-out detail, we offer most of the car care your vehicle will ever need — all in one place.

Steam exterior wash

High-temperature steam softens and lifts road grime, tar and Lagos dust without the harsh scrubbing that scratches paint. It uses a fraction of the water of a conventional wash and leaves a deep, streak-free finish.

  • Paint-safe, no swirl marks
  • Door jambs, sills & wheel arches included
  • Tyre & rim shine to finish
~90%Less water

Interior steam & vacuum

Steam sanitises seats, carpets, vents and touch-points — killing germs and lifting odours, not just masking them.

Upholstery & leather care

Engine bay cleaning

Careful steam degreasing of the engine bay — dirt and oil gone, sensitive electrics protected.

Full detailing

The complete inside-out treatment: exterior steam, interior deep clean, seat shampoo, polish and finishing. Your car, showroom-fresh.

Trained washers

A large, experienced team means your car is handled with care — and you're not waiting around, even when the yard is busy.

Steam washing uses high-temperature, low-pressure vapour to loosen dirt so it wipes away with minimal contact. Because there's no gritty sponge dragging across the surface, it's actually gentler on paint than a bucket wash — and it reaches vents, seams and crevices water can't.
